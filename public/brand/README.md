on third-party availability at runtime.

- `viadonau-logo.svg`: official viadonau website asset,
  <https://www.viadonau.org/_assets/28b34c7b2f06e1ac15b2ce6d7f3727c8/Images/viadonau-logo.svg>
- `dhk-logo.jpg`: official BMIMI DHK asset,
  <https://www.bmimi.gv.at/dam/jcr:65ed57f8-b98c-45b5-8297-3dd2a003a88d/dhk.jpg>

Both files were retrieved on 2026-07-30 and are displayed without modifying
their marks or colors.
